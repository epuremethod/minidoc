A `states` code block draws steps and the moves between them. Its first
lines are a grid: each word is a box, and a dot leaves a cell empty. After
a blank line, `a -> b label` is a move. A move between rows curves. A label
sits in the middle of its move, unless `{0.2}` says where, and
`{0.2 right}` puts it on the right of the way.
